# DS210-Project-Proposal
Place information on your datasest here

| Variable                      |  Description
|:----------------|:--------------------------------
|`course`                     | Degree student is or was enrolled in
|`previous_qualification`     | Previous education for student
|`mother_s_qualification`     | Mother's highest education
|`father_s_qualification`     | Father's highest education
|`admission_grade`            | Based on a 4.0 scale of student's admission grade
|`age_at_enrollment`          | Age student enrolled 
|`educational_special_needs`  | Student receives additional educational help due to learning disability
|`scholarship_holder`         | If student has a scholar ship
|`tuition_fees_up_to_date`    | Student pay tuition on time
|`target`                     | Weather a student Graduated, Dropped Out, or remained enrolled.
